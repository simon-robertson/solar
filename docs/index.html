A little perspective.

Explore the Sun, eight planets and our Moon, rendered as mathematical spheres in a WebGPU fragment shader.

Drag to orbit. Scroll to zoom. Select a world to fly closer. Press Space to pause, R to return to today, or Esc to return to the system view.

A beautiful approximation

Sizes and distances are compressed independently for visibility. Planets use JPL’s approximate Keplerian elements for 1800–2050; Earth uses the Earth–Moon barycentre. The Moon follows a simplified ellipse. This is an illustrative orrery, not an ephemeris or an N-body simulation. Dates stop at the model’s limits.

Made with a view of the real worlds

Textures by Solar System Scope, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Original 2K maps are resampled and mipmapped on upload. Orbital elements and planetary parameters from NASA / JPL Solar System Dynamics. Stars are procedural; atmosphere halos are artistic approximations. Planet textures include artistic colour adjustments and reconstructed terrain.
