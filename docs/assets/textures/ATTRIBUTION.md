Planet, Sun, Moon, Earth cloud/night and Saturn ring textures by **Solar System Scope**.

- Original download URLs: `https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/<filename>`

The original 2K files are included unmodified. At load time, maps are resampled into a
1024 × 512 GPU texture array and mipmapped. Ring data is resampled into a separate
GPU texture. The source maps are based on NASA imagery, with artistic adjustments
and some reconstructed terrain. They are illustrative rather than scientific data.
